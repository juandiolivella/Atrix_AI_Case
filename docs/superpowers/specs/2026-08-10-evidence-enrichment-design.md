# Evidence Enrichment Design

## Purpose

Add workflow Step 3 after all data-quality suggestions are approved. The screen makes the cleaned ASCO 2025 evidence usable before priorities are created.

## Experience

- Step 1 and 2 appear complete; **Enrich evidence** is active.
- The header confirms five approved data-quality corrections and six analysed files.
- A structured evidence map presents representative ASCO signals grouped by intelligence theme, linked sources, confidence, and a short evidence summary.
- Atrix proposes three enrichments: map signals to KITs/KIQs, cluster corroborating sources, and attach traceability/confidence metadata.
- Each suggestion can be accepted or edited for this browser session. The final CTA advances only after the enrichment review is complete.

## Validation

React and the Vercel static version include the enrichment title, structured evidence map, and accept/edit controls. Existing build, test, and lint checks remain green.
